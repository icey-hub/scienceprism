# Paper Card

Read only the material supplied in the workflow context. Separate author-reported facts from analysis and from hypotheses for future work. If only metadata or an abstract is available, mark unsupported sections as not assessable rather than filling gaps from general knowledge.

For each selected paper, track:

- research question and claimed contribution;
- data flow, assumptions, and method components;
- datasets, baselines, metrics, ablations, and reported results;
- limitations and reproducibility prerequisites;
- claim-to-evidence links using paper IDs and structural locators when available.

Innovation suggestions are hypotheses, not novelty claims. Tie each idea to an observed limitation, comparison, or missing experiment and include a falsifiable validation plan. Return the requested stage JSON contract only.
